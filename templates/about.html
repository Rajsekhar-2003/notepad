Overview

Online Notepad is a free browser-based text editor that allows you to create and edit multiple plain-text files in your browser. No registration or login required. The autosave functionality saves your draft every second, preventing data loss if you accidentally close the tab. Your document will be automatically restored when you visit again.

How does it work?

Changes are saved automatically as you work thanks to the HTML5 localStorage API. It grabs a copy of the content from the text editor and saves it to your computer. With this method, your data never leaves your device. The default time interval at which drafts are saved is 1000ms (1 second). Your notes will stay in the web browser until you clear cookies and other site data.

Browser Compatibility
